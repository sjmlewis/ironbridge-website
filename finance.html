Finance for UK SMEs ready to grow

Winning defence and security work often means spending before you are paid: on materials, staff, equipment and space, with long payment terms on top. We help established UK SMEs put together a clear case and arrange the finance to deliver.

Talk to us about finance

What we arrange

Working capital

To cover materials, labour and stock while a new contract ramps up.

Equipment and asset finance

To buy or lease machines, test equipment and vehicles, paid for over their working life.

Invoice finance

To release cash tied up in invoices on long payment terms.

Growth loans

For a new site, a new production line or a step up in capacity.

How it works

  1. A first conversation

    We talk through what the finance is for, how much is needed and by when.

  2. Preparing the case

    We help you pull together what lenders ask for: accounts, forecasts, the contract or order book, and the plan.

  3. Approaching lenders

    We approach lenders suited to your size, sector and need, and introduce you.

  4. Through to a decision

    We stay with you through the lenders’ questions until you have a decision.

Who it is for

UK limited companies, typically established SMEs with customers, filed accounts and a clear use for the money, including firms supplying defence, security and aerospace programmes.

Typical deal size: £250,000 to £10 million.

Good to know

We don’t lend money ourselves, and we never hold client money. We work with UK limited companies only.

Our fee is agreed with you in writing before we start.

Project record

Projects led by our founder, Stephen Lewis, in earlier roles. Most were made possible by finance he raised from defence companies and banks, to win exports, build capability or set up facilities. Client names are not shown.

Exports won

Aerospace components, Taiwan

Raised finance from an Italian aerospace company to link a Taiwanese manufacturer with a British aerospace manufacturer that wanted to outsource component work. Technology transfer, training and certification led to about $100 million of aerospace component exports to the UK over three years.

Steel, South Korea

Helped three Korean steelmakers export more than $200 million of steel to European and US defence companies, under five contracts over six years.

New exporters, South Africa

Raised finance from a European defence company to help three South African firms win new exports over three years: ship and oil-platform repair in Cape Town for foreign owners ($30 million), earth-moving equipment from Durban to Europe ($25 million) and gold jewellery from Cape Town to the United States ($26 million).

Agricultural exports, Thailand

Took over a US defence company’s commitment to the Thai Ministry of Commerce to generate $20 million of new Thai exports, with the financial penalty if they fell short, and agreed price and delivery with an international trading company as the new buyer of Thai agricultural products.

Steel, United Arab Emirates

Raised an improved financing package for a state-owned steel mill, secured on the purchase guarantees of a steel trader that agreed to sell the mill’s products through its international network, giving the mill a new long-term customer.

Regional jets, Brazil and the United States

Raised finance from a specialist aircraft finance bank for a start-up US airline’s regional jets, through a new offshore leasing company, on condition that the aircraft were ordered from Brazil. This helped a US defence company meet its commitment to grow Brazil’s high-tech exports.

Patrol vessels, Egypt

Raised the finance for a European shipyard’s sale of offshore patrol vessels to the Egyptian Navy: a state-owned Egyptian steel mill’s exports to the eurozone built up euros in escrow for the stage payments, and the Egyptian government repaid the mill in local currency.

Capability built

Maritime data fusion, United Arab Emirates

Raised finance from a German defence group for a UAE capability that fuses satellite communications with ship, drone, aircraft and land-based radar data, led locally by a UAE technology company. Negotiated the project’s terms with the UAE government authority.

Satellite imagery, Denmark

Raised finance from a US defence company for a UK SME that analyses satellite imagery and video to set up in Denmark with a local partner, having agreed with Denmark’s head of Arctic security that its capabilities would help meet Arctic security needs. Then managed the project for three years, as the two firms developed intelligence services for the Danish government and a new product for international markets.

Defence engineering, India

Raised finance from a US defence company for a technology cooperation arrangement with agencies of India’s defence research organisation, building an integrated military product engineering capability while keeping it clear of international sanctions on nuclear technology.

Aerospace engineering, Kuwait

Raised finance from a US defence company for a European technology group to give a Kuwaiti university the aerospace engineering and quality-control capability its expanding engineering faculties needed, under an agreement negotiated with a Kuwaiti government agency.

Universities and research, Romania

Raised about €20 million of technology products, donated by an international technology company, for Romanian universities and research centres, to improve education and cooperation with local industry, with the backing of a major defence company it already worked with.

Shipbuilding, Turkey

Raised finance for new ship construction at a shipyard in Tuzla, through a new structure worth about €90 million that included mezzanine debt from a foreign defence supplier. Also trained Turkey’s defence procurement agency in ship finance.

Facilities set up

Engineering company, Saudi Arabia

Raised finance from a US defence company for the set-up of a joint venture between a Saudi government-owned company and a foreign partner: an engineering company commercialising Saudi intellectual property in the oil and gas sector, with US$90 million of foreign equity.

SME engineering centre, South Africa

Raised finance from a defence company to set up a defence SME engineering centre with South Africa’s state-owned research and development organisation in Pretoria, to train SMEs and bring their technologies into key government industry sectors.

Technology centre, Finland

Raised finance from a US defence company to set up a self-financing research and development centre at Finland’s main engineering university, for long-term defence product development projects for Finnish industry and SMEs.
