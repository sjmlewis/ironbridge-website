Finance for UK SMEs ready to grow

Winning defence and security work often means spending before you are paid: on materials, staff, equipment and space, with long payment terms on top. We help established UK SMEs put together a clear case and arrange the finance to deliver.

Talk to us about finance

What we arrange

Working capital

To cover materials, labour and stock while a new contract ramps up.

Equipment and asset finance

To buy or lease machines, test equipment and vehicles, paid for over their working life.

Invoice finance

To release cash tied up in invoices on long payment terms.

Growth loans

For a new site, a new production line or a step up in capacity.

How it works

  1. A first conversation

    We talk through what the finance is for, how much is needed and by when.

  2. Preparing the case

    We help you pull together what lenders ask for: accounts, forecasts, the contract or order book, and the plan.

  3. Approaching lenders

    We approach lenders suited to your size, sector and need, and introduce you.

  4. Through to a decision

    We stay with you through the lenders’ questions until you have a decision.

Who it is for

UK limited companies, typically established SMEs with customers, filed accounts and a clear use for the money, including firms supplying defence, security and aerospace programmes.

Typical deal size: £250,000 to £10 million.

Good to know

We don’t lend money ourselves, and we never hold client money. We work with UK limited companies only.

Our fee is agreed with you in writing before we start.

Financing examples

Examples of finance arranged by our founder, Stephen Lewis, in earlier roles.

  • Shipbuilding, Turkey. A new financing structure for new ship construction at a Turkish shipyard, including mezzanine debt from a foreign supplier: about €90 million.
  • Patrol vessels, Egypt. Payment arrangements for a European shipyard’s sale of offshore patrol vessels, with export earnings held in escrow to meet the stage payments.
  • Regional jets, United States and Brazil. Finance for a start-up US airline’s regional jets, arranged with a specialist aircraft finance bank through a new offshore leasing company.
  • Steel, United Arab Emirates. An offtake agreement that gave a state-owned steel mill an improved financing package, secured on the buyer’s purchase guarantees.
  • Technology SME, Denmark. Finance for a UK technology SME to set up in Denmark with a local partner, followed by three years of project management.
  • Bank facilities. As a director of Stemcor Trade Finance, shared responsibility for the group’s US$2 billion of credit facilities from more than 50 trade finance banks.
