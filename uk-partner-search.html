Find UK companies that can make your parts or develop your technology

The UK Defence Partner Database is a self-service search for overseas defence and security companies. It covers UK manufacturers, technology companies, SMEs, universities and research organisations, and shows the evidence behind every match.

How a search works

  1. Upload your parts list

    A spreadsheet of plain, unclassified descriptions. No drawings, part numbers or controlled data are needed.

  2. Leave out your competitors

    Name the groups you compete with and their companies are removed from your results. Anything removed stays listed, so you can bring it back.

  3. Review the matches

    Results for each part are grouped by readiness, each with a fit score, a reason in plain English and links to the company’s own published information.

  4. Check the firms you pick

    See financial health from filed accounts, the quality approvals and certificates on record, and ownership and security information for your shortlist.

  5. Make contact in your own name

    Prepare a first-stage capability enquiry for each firm. As an extra safeguard, the platform removes part numbers, NSNs, drawing numbers and supplier names automatically. You send it yourself, and replies come straight to you.

  6. Plan your visit

    The visit planner sets out a day-by-day trip to the firms you choose, by train and hire car, with rough costs and the route in Google Maps.

You can also search the directory on its own, by company name, capability, standard or place, and keep your own partner lists.

What you get for each part

Every firm suggested carries one of these labels, a fit score, a reason in plain English and links to its own published information.

For each organisation, the database draws together, where available: capabilities and products, facilities, certificates, size, financial health, ownership, location, business contacts, research activity and past public contract awards.

Anything not on record is marked “not recorded, confirm with the supplier”. It is never treated as a fail.
