How we work

Evidence, not claims

Every match shows its reasons and the evidence behind them. Where something is not on record, we say so rather than guess.

Real UK capability

Matches are organisations that make or develop things in the UK. Distributors, resellers and sales-only offices are not shown as manufacturers.

Stability checked

For each firm you shortlist, the search shows its financial health from filed accounts, any insolvency or overdue accounts, who owns it and the certificates on record.

You deal direct

You contact UK organisations yourself, in your own name, and their replies come straight back to you. We don’t sit in the middle.
