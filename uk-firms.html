Partnering and capability development for UK defence firms

Overseas defence companies that sell to the UK are looking for capable UK partners, and not only among the firms linked to what they sell. Our platform lets UK defence and security firms, from micro firms to medium-sized ones, check they are ready and put their projects in front of those companies.

The opportunity

Overseas defence companies that sell to the UK want capable UK partners: firms that can develop new technology with them, take on new engineering work and build new capability here.

The partner they need is not always linked to what they sell. A large overseas group has many divisions and product lines, and a firm whose capability fits any part of that wider business can be the right partner: a space firm, say, for a group better known for its helicopters.

For the right UK firm, partnering can bring engineering support, technology and finance from the overseas company, and capability that lasts.

What overseas companies look for

Something genuinely new

Work that would not happen otherwise: a new product, new capability or new technology, not existing business under a new name.

A credible UK value

Real work, skills and capability in the UK, with a value the overseas company can stand behind.

A firm that is ready

The approvals the work needs, sound finances, clear ownership and the right security arrangements.

A case put their way

Set out the way that company wants to receive it, and put to the right people in the right part of the group.

How the platform helps

Our platform helps overseas defence companies find UK partners. UK firms have their own side of it.

See how you appear

Check the record that overseas defence companies see for your firm, and suggest corrections. Nothing changes until they have been checked.

Check you are ready

A private check of where your firm stands on what overseas partners look for: approvals, finances, ownership, security, controlled technology and capacity. It shows what is in place and what is worth looking at.

Put a project forward

Describe what you want to develop and the help you are looking for, from engineering support and technology to finance, work, joint development or new markets, in as much detail as you choose: the more you give, the easier it is for an overseas company to decide. You confirm it contains nothing classified and nothing that would need an export licence to share. Once approved, it appears on a notice board that only overseas companies see.

If a project needs finance, we can help arrange that too. Finance for UK SMEs
