Partnering and capability development for UK defence firms

Overseas defence companies that sell to the UK are looking for capable UK partners, and not only among the firms linked to what they sell. We help UK defence and security firms, from micro firms to medium-sized ones, see where their capability fits and put a strong case to the right overseas company.

The opportunity

Overseas defence companies that sell to the UK want capable UK partners: firms that can develop new technology with them, take on new engineering work and build new capability here.

The partner they need is not always linked to what they sell. A large overseas group has many divisions and product lines, and a firm whose capability fits any part of that wider business can be the right partner: a space firm, say, for a group better known for its helicopters.

For the right UK firm, partnering can bring engineering support, technology and finance from the overseas company, and capability that lasts.

What overseas companies look for

Something genuinely new

Work that would not happen otherwise: a new product, new capability or new technology, not existing business under a new name.

A credible UK value

Real work, skills and capability in the UK, with a value the overseas company can stand behind.

A firm that is ready

The approvals the work needs, sound finances, clear ownership and the right security arrangements.

A case put their way

Set out the way that company wants to receive it, and put to the right people in the right part of the group.

How we help

Finding the fit

We help you see which overseas groups, and which parts of those groups, could want what your firm does.

Shaping the case

Every overseas company wants something different, from a short email to a full business plan. We work with you on what your firm could take on and what it would need, and set it out the way that company expects.

Putting it forward

We take your case to the overseas company and put it in front of the people who can act on it.

If the work needs finance, we can help arrange that too. Finance for UK SMEs
