About us

We are a UK company serving the defence and security industry. We run the UK Defence Partner Database, a self-service search that helps overseas companies find capable UK partners, and we help UK SMEs arrange the finance they need to grow.

Founder

Stephen Lewis is the company’s founder and director. He has been deeply involved in the defence sector for more than 25 years, working with companies and governments in nearly 30 countries across Europe, the Middle East, Asia, Africa and the Americas.

His background is in finance: structured, asset, trade and commodities finance, export finance and banking.

From 2001 to 2015 he was a director of Stemcor Trade Finance, the trade finance arm of an international metals and commodities group. There he worked on project, structured and asset finance, and shared responsibility for the group’s US$2 billion of credit facilities from more than 50 trade finance banks.

Since 2014 he has arranged finance for UK defence and technology SMEs, working with banks, UK Export Finance and insurers. He began his career at PricewaterhouseCoopers.

Contact

For a demonstration of the UK partner search, tell us your name, organisation and country, and the kind of parts or technology you are looking for.

For finance, tell us what the finance is for, roughly how much, and by when.

Please don’t send programme, technical or classified information by email.

Privacy

We use the details you send us only to reply and to deal with your enquiry. We don’t sell or share them. You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time.

The UK Defence Partner Database holds publicly available business information about organisations, including business contact details. If your details appear and you would like them corrected or removed, contact us.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
